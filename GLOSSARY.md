# Grievance Redressal

Grievance redressal covers seeking a remedy from a company for a problem with its goods, services, or handling of a complaint. Refund requests are the first type of grievance addressed here.

## Language

**Grievance case**:
A particular problem a person wants a company to remedy, together with the facts and correspondence relating to that problem.
_Avoid_: Refund case as the name for all grievance cases

**Refund request**:
A request to return money paid, in full or in part, as the desired remedy for a grievance.

**Complaint draft**:
A proposed message describing a grievance and requesting a remedy, which has not been sent to the company.
_Avoid_: Submitted complaint when referring to a draft

**Research source**:
A company-published page used to establish its policy, complaint procedure or support contact. Its contents are distinct from the person's account of what happened.

**Policy applicability**:
Whether a published policy covers the circumstances of a particular grievance case. Finding a policy alone does not establish applicability.

**Follow-up draft**:
A proposed message continuing an existing grievance after an earlier complaint, such as a response to the company's reply.

**Resolved case**:
A grievance case the person explicitly considers satisfactorily addressed.
_Avoid_: Sent or drafted as synonyms for resolved

**Closed without resolution**:
A grievance case the person has stopped pursuing without considering it satisfactorily addressed.
